🌏 About Me
✈️ Good Times, Far Places
✨ Grace · @theGFsadvise ✨

I'm Grace — a curious soul with a passion for exploration and a deep love for discovering the world's hidden gems. My journey began with a simple belief: travel is more than just seeing new places; it's about collecting moments, flavors, and stories.

From the cherry blossom-lined streets of Tokyo to the bustling night markets of Seoul and the ancient wonders of Beijing, every destination has a story waiting to be told. I don't just plan trips — I curate experiences that stay with you long after the journey ends.

Travel is not just about seeing new places. It's about collecting moments, tasting local flavors, and connecting with people who make this world feel like home.

🌏 Why GF: Global Footprints?
The "GF" stands for "Global Footprints" — because that's exactly what I believe travel should be. Whether you're a solo adventurer, a couple, or a group of friends, I'm here to help you turn your travel dreams into reality.

✨ What I offer:
Handcrafted, day-by-day itineraries Curated hotel recommendations Local food guides Instagram-worthy spots Insider tips only locals know Global Footprints community

Whether you're planning your first big trip or your fiftieth, I'm here to make every journey effortless, memorable, and full of good times.
